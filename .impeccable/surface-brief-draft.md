## Direction contract

**THESIS:** An evidence-first intelligence desk: the dashboard behaves like a calm editorial control surface, not a wall of equal KPI cards. It refuses the standard dense SaaS dashboard where every metric has the same weight. Operational data is grouped by decisions: what changed, why it matters, and where to inspect evidence.

**OWN-WORLD:** White canvas and near-black ink dominate. Hairlines organize dense controls. Signature coral, forest, cream, peach, mint and dark-navy surfaces appear as deliberate analytical moments, never scattered accents. Typography stays regular/medium, with hierarchy from scale and spacing. Controls use 6/10/12px radii and flat surfaces; shadows are exceptional.

**STORY:** A user lands on a concise state of the monitored conversation, adjusts project/platform/time filters, sees the movement that matters, then drills into posts, sentiment, keywords, competitors or complaints. The same shell makes investigation, reporting and automation feel like one continuous workflow instead of separate tools.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Desktop opens with a 64px utility top bar, a compact 252px grouped navigation rail, then a wide white work canvas. The title/filter band is quiet. The first analytical composition is asymmetric: one large engagement narrative/chart region on the left, a narrow vertical signal column on the right, and a compact KPI strip integrated into the same editorial grid rather than six floating cards. The primary action is a dark 'Explore evidence' control adjacent to the most important insight, not in global chrome.

**FORM:** Preserve familiar admin-dashboard affordances but compose them as an editorial evidence desk. Grounded structure selected for build: asymmetric evidence-first command center with fixed filter grammar and progressively denser investigation bands. Surface concept seed `d70a4653` was run as required; the user's pinned source/workflow and Airtable design direction override unrelated world challengers.
